Hello and welcome! My name is Jiayi Zhang (he/him). I am a PhD candidate at [The University of Hong Kong (HKU)](https://www.hku.hk/), advised by [Chenxiong Qian](https://i.cs.hku.hk/~cqian/). I earned my MSc(Comp) in Computer Science with distinction from HKU in 2023. I got my dual BSc(Eng)(Hons.) and B.M. degrees in E-Commerce Engineering with Law with First Class Honours from [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications](https://english.bupt.edu.cn/) and [Queen Mary University of London](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/) in 2022. My research interests include ML for game security, cheat and anti-cheat in FPS games, and other emerging or practical topics related to video games.
